## ✦ Overview
Personal portfolio website of **Yousef Ward (يوسف ورد)**, presenting visual craft across three connected pillars:
- **01. Filmmaking & Cinema (صناعة الأفلام والسينما):** Drama sets (*El Ekhteyar*, *Cairo Kabul*), broadcast television (*Sahranen*), and spiritual kinetic studies (*Mawlawya*).
- **02. Still Photography (التصوير الفوتوغرافي):** Editorial fashion lookbooks (*Clove I & II*, *Kojak Studio*), 35mm light-sculpted studio portraits, and Cairo street transitions.
- **03. Graphic Design & Key Art (التصميم الجرافيكي والبوستر):** Cultural brand systems (*Diwan*, *Mawlawya*), architectural book covers & typography, and exhibition posters.

---

## 🛠 Technology
- **Pure Vanilla Web Standards:** 100% semantic HTML5, modern CSS3, and modular vanilla JavaScript.
- **Zero Dependencies:** No frameworks (React/Vue), no npm, no bundlers, no build step.
- **Bilingual Architecture:** Intentional Arabic RTL (default) and English LTR layouts with real-time toggle.
- **A+B Hybrid Visual System:** Rigid modular grid & typographic scale contrast (Direction A) combined with 2.39:1 widescreen narrative blocks, viewfinder brackets, and obsidian dark mode (Direction B).
- **Interactive Features:** Hash-based SPA routing, custom cursor physics, and full-screen cinematic lightbox.
