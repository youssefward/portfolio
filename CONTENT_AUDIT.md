## 1. Executive Summary & Audit Methodology

The Content Audit evaluates all existing and potential projects to ensure that:
1. **Credibility is 100% airtight:** No invented claims, roles, or unconfirmed awards.
2. **Assets are verified on disk:** Every selected project is backed by high-resolution visual evidence already residing in `E:\portfolio`.
3. **Core Positioning is reinforced:** Cinematography-led, design-informed — highlighting visual concept, lighting, composition, and art direction.

### Status Definitions (Internal Workflow Labels):
- **`SELECTED` (جاهز ومعتمد للعرض الرئيسي):** High visual quality, strong evidence on disk, clear role, ready for live portfolio or featured case study.
- **`DEVELOP` (واعد ويحتاج تطوير أصول أو تدقيق):** High potential, but missing high-res exports, client verification, or written narrative before public publishing.
- **`ARCHIVE` (مؤرشف / لا يُنصح بنشره حالياً):** Redundant, lacks sufficient visual assets, unverified credit, or outside current positioning priority.
